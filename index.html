01 — Research

What I want to figure out

Primary interest

Source bias in multimodal recommendation

Ranking systems have been shown to prefer AI-generated text and images over semantically equivalent human-created content. I want to know whether this happens in multimodal recommenders when only one modality of an item is machine-generated, where the preference lives, and whether it can be removed post hoc — without retraining large vision-language backbones.

Robust vision under data scarcity

Rare foreground classes, multi-spectral inputs, and evaluation protocols that reflect real deployment instead of headline accuracy.
