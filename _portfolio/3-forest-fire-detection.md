**Type:** research proposal (UNSW GSOE9011 Research Skills, 2025). This is a proposal; no experiments have been run yet.

**Motivation.** General-purpose satellite fire detectors lose accuracy and raise more false alarms in New South Wales, where oil-rich eucalyptus forests, complex terrain and distinctive fire behaviour differ from the conditions they were trained on.

**Proposed approach.** Build and evaluate a transformer-based model on open satellite data to capture spatio-temporal fire patterns specific to NSW, and benchmark it against CNN-based methods using historical NSW fire datasets. The proposal sets target levels for detection accuracy, false-alarm rate and alert latency.
