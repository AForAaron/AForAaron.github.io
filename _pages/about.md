I hold a Master of Information Technology (Artificial Intelligence specialisation) from UNSW Sydney and a Bachelor of Engineering in Software Engineering from Changchun University of Science and Technology. Since graduating, I have been working as an IT engineer in Shanxi, China. I am currently **seeking PhD opportunities**, and my start date is flexible.

Research interests
======
My background is in **deep learning and computer vision**. Through course-based research projects at UNSW, I have worked on pixel-level segmentation under extreme class imbalance and on fine-grained image classification. In both projects I relied on controlled model comparisons and per-class error analysis to understand *why* a model improves, not only *how much*.

Going forward, I am interested in **trustworthy multimodal AI**, in particular:

* **Source bias in multimodal recommendation.** Ranking systems have been shown to prefer AI-generated text and images over semantically equivalent human-created content. I am interested in whether this preference appears in multimodal recommenders when only one modality of an item is machine-generated, where in the pipeline it resides (visual encoder, fusion layer or interaction history), and whether it can be mitigated post hoc without retraining large vision-language backbones.
* **Robust visual learning under data scarcity.** This covers rare foreground classes, multi-spectral inputs, and evaluation protocols that reflect real deployment conditions.

I am also open to related directions within a supervisor's group.

Selected projects
======
* **Semantic segmentation of standing dead trees in aerial RGB+NIR imagery** (UNSW, 2025): a controlled comparison of FCN-ResNet50, SegFormer and DeepLabV3+ on a 6-channel input. SegFormer achieved the best mIoU (0.651) and DeepLabV3+ the best F2-score (0.594). [Details](/portfolio/dead-tree-segmentation/)
* **Attention-guided EfficientNetV2 + DenseNet ensemble for fashion item classification** (UNSW, 2024): accuracy improved from 0.917 (ResNet-50 baseline) to 0.972, with most of the gain on visually similar categories. [Details](/portfolio/fashion-classification/)
* **Real-time forest fire detection for NSW bushland** (UNSW, 2025, research proposal): a proposed transformer-based detector on open satellite data, to be benchmarked against CNN-based methods. [Details](/portfolio/forest-fire-detection/)
